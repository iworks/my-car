== Description ==

**My Car** is a car manager for WordPress — build a garage of your vehicles, keep a digital passport for each one, track the mileage and record every service visit. Everything is managed from the admin in one place and presented on the frontend with clean, configurable data tables.

= Vehicles =

* **Cars post type** — add every vehicle you own or manage with a photo, description and a **Vehicle Digital Passport**: VIN, license plate, production year, engine capacity, engine power and, for electrified cars, battery capacity and electric range.
* **Taxonomies** — organize cars by brand & model (hierarchical), body type and engine type.
* **Engine-aware passport** — electric cars don't show engine capacity and petrol cars don't show battery range: which passport fields apply is configured per engine type.

= Mileage =

* **Mileage log** — every car has its own mileage log with date and odometer entries, managed from a handy modal window directly in the cars list.

= Service records =

* **Service Records post type** — log inspections, part replacements and other service events with date, mileage, workshop, cost and status (done / planned).
* **Service types** — tag each event with predefined service types (engine oil, filters, brake pads, timing belt, and more).
* **History per car** — every service record is linked to its car, so the full service history is always available.

= Frontend presentation =

* Each car page displays a **Vehicle Digital Passport** table, the **Mileage Log** and the **Service Records** history.
* Sensitive data is under your control: VIN, license plate, mileage log and service records each have their own visibility setting — show to owner only, show to everyone, or hide.

= Predefined data =

* Import ready-made term sets with one click: popular brands with their models, body types, engine types and service types.
* Language-aware presets — when your site runs in a supported language (e.g. Polish), translated terms are offered automatically.

== Installation ==

There are 3 ways to install this plugin:

= The super-easy way =
1. Navigate to WPA > the Plugins and click the `Add New` button.
1. Search for `My Car`.
1. Click to install.
1. Activate the plugin.
1. A new menu `Cars` will appear in your Admin.

= The easy way =

1. Download the plugin (.zip file) on the right column of this page
1. Navigate to WPA > the Plugins and click the `Add New` button.
1. Select button `Upload Plugin`.
1. Upload the .zip file you just downloaded.
1. Activate the plugin.
1. A new menu `Cars` will appear in your Admin.

= The old and reliable way (FTP) =

1. Upload `my-car` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory
1. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
1. A new menu `Cars` will appear in your Admin.
